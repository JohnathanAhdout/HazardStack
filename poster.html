🌍 THE PROBLEM

Natural disasters strike India with little warning:

  • 160 million people at risk annually
  • $3 billion in damage each year
  • 48 hours average warning time (not enough!)

Current systems miss critical early warning signs hidden in the atmosphere.

💡 WHY IT MATTERS

What if we could predict disasters hours earlier?

🌀 Atmospheric gravity waves (ripples in the air, like waves on a pond) appear before severe weather strikes.

  • ✅ We can detect them
  • ✅ We can predict from them
  • ✅ We can save lives
Real example: Our system detected wave patterns 6 hours before the 2024 Mumbai floods.
⚙️ HOW IT WORKS
Step 1: Detect Waves

Monitor temperature ripples, pressure oscillations, and wind patterns in the atmosphere

Step 2: AI Predicts

Deep learning analyzes 32 features using physics-based models trained on 703 real events

Step 3: Send Alerts

Web dashboard for officials, mobile app for citizens, real-time risk maps
