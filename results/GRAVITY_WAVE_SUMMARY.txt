NEW OPTIMIZATION: Atmospheric Gravity Wave Detection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Atmospheric gravity waves are ripples in the atmosphere caused by
disturbances like mountains, convection, or weather fronts. They
create visible patterns (lenticular clouds) and are critical
precursors to convective precipitation and extreme rainfall.
